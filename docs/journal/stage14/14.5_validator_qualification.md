Plan reference: B4.1 to B4.5 (v2 p.9-10); P3.1 to P3.3 (p.20); §9 human workload (p.43); first sprint item 5 (p.48).
Handbook: `docs/validation/v2/validator_handbook_v2.md`. Materials: `docs/validation/v2/materials/v1/`.

Compute: CPU only; committed data only. No model, GPU or Slurm.

This stage builds instruments and candidate materials. It validates nothing: the plan states that an agent's
self-review is not independent expert validation (p.43) and that validators must not be the people who wrote a family
(B4.1). Every output is marked CANDIDATE and every human decision is left PENDING.

**What:** The planted-defect injector (`src/physmon/validation/defect_injection.py`) implements the ten types in v2
B4.4 as deterministic edits with an applicability check, each tested to make exactly its declared edit
(`tests/v2/test_defect_injection_v2.py`, 13 tests). Two are Pass 2 only (the equation edit and the precision edit);

**What reading the output changed [V].** My first rules produced several poor defects, found by reading real items
before anything was committed, and fixed:
1. `invalid_parameter_domain` negated "a temperature increase of 200 K": a negative change is a valid cooling. The
   rule now negates only a mass or an amount of substance.
2. `insufficient_boundary_condition` left "which is." (an ungrammatical fragment that a reviewer would flag for the
   wrong reason). It is now an explicit per-template edit, limited to the three templates whose law needs the
   initial condition (a collision with a body at rest, a ball dropped from rest, a cart released from rest). A fourth
   candidate (`CM_A_008`) restates "(0.0 m/s)", so deleting the words would not be a defect and it is excluded.
3. `failed_transformation_identity` scaled all four renderings together, which leaves them equivalent to each other and
   invisible to a blind Pass 1 review. It now alters rendering 1 only, so the renderings disagree.
4. `contradictory_distractor_statement` restated the value without its unit; it now carries the unit.
5. `missing_assumption` deleted "frictionless" from prompts that give the acceleration or force directly, where the
   problem stays solvable (not a defect). It is now an explicit allow-list: the frictionless ramp (`CM_B_UM_047`,
   energy conservation) and the three ideal-gas families (`TH_B_UM_006` to `008`). Ten of the 13 candidates were
   excluded after reading each governing equation.
**Limit:** these are text edits whose physical effect a physicist must confirm; the bank file has an empty
`physicist_confirmation` block for every item.
