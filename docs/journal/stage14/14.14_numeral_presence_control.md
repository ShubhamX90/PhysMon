### J14.14-001 · 2026-10-18 · decision
kind: decision
**What:** Build the matched control that J14.12-002 found missing: score, under every cell prompt, each numeral that appears in the prompt as a candidate answer (target unit, `answer_line` wrapper, written exactly as in the prompt), tagged by role, together with a matched absent control (same shape, not in the prompt, not within 1% of the gold).
**Why:** the pilot showed that a same-unit cue numeral becomes a much more likely answer and that the boost depends on unit compatibility (J14.9-006). It could not show whether this is specific to an irrelevant cue or a general property of any number present in the prompt, including the numbers the problem needs. The presence effect (log-probability of a present numeral minus that of its matched absent control, under the same prompt) can be compared across roles: relevant input, isolated cue with the same unit, isolated cue with an incompatible unit, metadata, and relevant reference (the role-reversal cell).
**Design [V]:** the plan reuses the job format of the pilot planner so the existing runner can score it; the candidate string is the job's `gold_text` and the real gold is kept in `true_gold_value` (stated in `candidate_note`). Controls come from a per-numeral seeded generator (seed 42). Paired contrasts are within a prompt (cue effect minus the mean effect of the relevant inputs of the same prompt) and between the same-unit and incompatible-unit cells, which share the cue digits.
**Limits stated before the run:** matched shape does not match digit frequency, so a small presence effect is read against the dtype noise floor (0.1 to 0.3 nats) and against the spread of controls; the numerals in a prompt are not independent; ten structures.
**Tests [V]:** `test_numeral_presence_s154.py` 6 passed (numeral extraction, control shape and distance, determinism, one control per present numeral, role tags, no decoys, directory refusal); `test_numeral_presence_analysis_s155.py` 5 passed on synthetic jobs and records with known effects (presence effect, roles, paired contrasts, NOT_EVALUATED, join errors, outputs).
