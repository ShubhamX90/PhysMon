### J14.12-001 · 2026-10-18 · decision
kind: decision
**What:** Extract what the records already say about copying versus relevance, without a job, and state what they cannot say.
**Why:** J14.9-006 showed that the boost depends on unit compatibility; the decision table asks that the copying account be tested before any relevance-gating language. The records contain one clean contrast and one unmatched one.
**The clean contrast:** the same-unit near cell and the incompatible-unit near cell share the gold string, the decoy string and the cue digits (checked per item; items with different cue numerals would be excluded and counted: none were); only the unit written in the prompt differs.
**The unmatched one:** the `role_reversal` cell makes the cue quantity relevant but changes the question (the answer is this value minus the value for system B), so its generation-level copy rate is not comparable with the isolated cells. A matched relevant-role control (the same numeral, the same question, a relevant role) does not exist in the records.

### J14.12-002 · 2026-10-18 · result
kind: result
**Tests [V]:** `test_copying_vs_relevance_s151.py`, 6 passed on synthetic records (unit modulation, exclusion of items with different cue numerals, copy rates, Wilson interval, role reversal, outputs).
**Unit modulation [V], same-unit near minus incompatible-unit near, ten structures, cluster bootstrap:** decoy shift Qwen +9.19 [5.75, 13.21] (10 of 10 structures up), Llama +3.36 [1.21, 5.85] (9 of 10); gold shift Qwen +0.88 [-0.60, 2.66], Llama +0.95 [-0.31, 2.34] (intervals include zero); R_alt shift Qwen -8.31 [-10.99, -5.59], Llama -2.41 [-3.76, -1.20].
So the same decoy string becomes more likely when the prompt's unit matches the requested quantity, with the gold's likelihood not detectably changed.
**Generations equal to the cue numeral [V] (parsed generations):** Qwen: same-unit cells 15 of 75 (near high overlap 4 of 20, near low overlap 3 of 15, moderate 5 of 20, far 3 of 20), incompatible-unit 0 of 20, numeric metadata 0 of 20, convertible unit 0 of 16. Llama: same-unit cells 2 of 75 (all in near low overlap, 2 of 15), incompatible-unit 0 of 20, numeric metadata 0 of 20, convertible unit 2 of 16.
So Qwen turns the likelihood boost into the generated answer in about one in five same-unit cases and never when the unit differs, while Llama shows a likelihood boost (+4 nats) that almost never reaches its generation.
**Role reversal [V], not matched:** Qwen 0 of 18 parsed generations equal the cue numeral, 1 correct, 15 of 18 with the negative sign of the gold; Llama 3 of 20 equal the cue numeral, 0 correct, 0 of 20 with the sign of the gold (it gives positive differences). The model that copies the isolated same-unit numeral (Qwen) does not copy it when the question asks for a difference; the question differs, so this is not a test of relevance.
**Reading [I]:** (a) the likelihood effect is modulated by unit compatibility in both models; (b) its behavioural expression differs strongly between the models (Qwen yes, Llama almost no); (c) the data do not say whether a numeral in a relevant role is boosted the same way, because no record scores the same candidate in a relevant-role cell with the same question. The decision-table row 4 question stays open.
**Not done:** the matched relevant-role control. It needs new scoring: the cue numeral and the unit-compatible candidates scored in the same cells where the numeral is metadata, isolated, and relevant, under one question. It belongs to the redesigned pilot (Stage 14.13), not to a patch of this one.
