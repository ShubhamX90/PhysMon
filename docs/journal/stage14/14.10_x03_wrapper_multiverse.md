### J14.10-001 · 2026-10-18 · decision
kind: decision
**What:** Run X03 on the ten v2 structures (211 feasible cells, both primary dense models) under all seven frozen wrappers of the Stage 14.3 contract: `answer_line`, `plain`, `boxed`, `json`, `sentence`, `decimal_plain`, `scientific`. The plan has 2,954 jobs (1,477 per model; 665 decoy-defined per model).
**Deviation from the card, stated plainly:** the card names 60 to 100 families drawn from the 140 original families. I use the ten v2 structures because their cues are factorially controlled, they have exact gold and decoy strings under every wrapper and the anchor-matched decoy score exists for them; the legacy families have none of that. The legacy-family version of X03 is not done. This version is a discovery design on ten structures, so it is precision-limited like the pilot.
**Sampling rule frozen before any wrapper score exists:** every feasible cell, no selection. The `answer_line` scores of the pilot exist (jobs 399969 and 400932) and are not used to choose anything. The plan is committed before the job.
**Frozen analysis (decided now, before the wrapper outputs):**
1. Primary quantity: the anchor-matched shift of R_alt from the no-cue prompt (Stage 14.9) in each decoy cell, per wrapper, by structure; and the reference-free range D_lp of the gold log-probability across cells that share the gold string, per wrapper.
2. Wrapper-by-nuisance interaction: for each structure, the shift under a wrapper minus the shift under `answer_line`, then the mean over structures with the cluster bootstrap over structures (2,000 resamples, seed 42), as in 14.8 and 14.9.
3. **Dominance criterion:** a wrapper dominates the cue effect if, for the same-unit near cell, its wrapper-minus-`answer_line` interval excludes zero and the absolute difference is at least half of the `answer_line` shift. **Invariance criterion:** none of the six differences has an interval that excludes zero; this means "no detectable wrapper dependence at ten structures", not equivalence.
4. Secondary: answer-token counts per wrapper (from the plan), parse rate and correctness per wrapper, per-token and total gold log-likelihood, dtype noise floor from J14.9 used as the reading scale (0.1 to 0.3 nats). First-token versus full-sequence score and value-only versus value-plus-unit likelihood from the card are **not** computed in this version (the raw records keep per-token log-probabilities, so they can be added later).
5. Missing results (parse failure, mismatch) are NOT_EVALUATED, reported by wrapper and model, never zero.
**No-go response (card):** if a wrapper dominates, the interpretation is tied to output elicitation and is not a universal physics vulnerability.

### J14.10-002 · 2026-10-18 · result
kind: result
**Tokenizer audit [V], from the committed plan (source commit `1ed8be2`):** 7 wrappers x 211 jobs per model; no `DUPLICATE_SPECIAL_TOKEN` and no `BOUNDARY_MISMATCH` for any gold or decoy string under either tokenizer. Mean gold-answer length in tokens, Llama / Qwen: `plain` 4.6 / 6.2, `answer_line` 7.5 / 9.1, `decimal_plain` 7.5 / 9.1, `boxed` 8.5 / 10.1,
`sentence` 9.5 / 11.1, `scientific` 9.6 / 11.1, `json` 14.5 / 16.1. So the wrappers differ by up to a factor of three in scored length, which is why raw sums are compared only within a wrapper and never across wrappers.
**Note [V]:** `decimal_plain` and `answer_line` give identical gold and decoy strings here (the values are already plain decimals) and differ only in the instruction, so their comparison isolates the instruction text from the target string.
Tests: `test_pilot_s147.py`, 13 passed, including a multi-wrapper plan (unique ids, `answer_line` ids unchanged, parsing every gold string under every wrapper recovers the same value and unit) and a runner pass under `boxed`. The default plan still reproduces the committed pilot manifest byte for byte.
