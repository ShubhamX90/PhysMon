### J14.17-001 · 2026-10-21 · decision
kind: decision
**What:** Build the control that J14.14-005 said was missing: a presence control whose absent numerals are as plausible as the numeral they are compared with.
**Why [V]:** the cue numerals of the near cells lie 0.1% to 0.8% from the gold (measured over the 10 batch-A structures: median 0.3%; the moderate cell 30%; the far cell 400%). The Stage 14.14 control was any same-shape numeral that is absent from the prompt and not within 1% of the gold, so a near cue was compared with numerals that are farther from the answer than it is. A model that finds near-gold numbers probable would show a large "presence effect" even if it never used the prompt. The Stage 14.14 contrasts therefore cannot separate presence from plausibility. [I] this is the main reason the 8 nats for Qwen are not yet evidence of copying.
**Design [V]** (implemented in `s159_matched_distance_plan.py`; seed 42):
1. Every numeral of every feasible cell prompt of both batches (S01 to S20) is a present candidate, written with the target unit in the `answer_line` wrapper as in Stage 14.14.
2. Up to four matched controls per numeral: numerals of the same shape (same count of integer digits and decimals) that are absent from the prompt and whose distance from the gold, d = |ln(v / gold)|, is within a factor of 2 of the numeral's own distance (relaxed to a factor of 4 only if fewer than two exist), two above and two below the gold where possible; a negative gold is compared by magnitude. A numeral gets no control, and is NOT_EVALUATED, if it lies within 0.1% of the gold (it would be a correct answer, not an equally plausible wrong one), if its gold is zero, or if no numeral of its shape exists at that distance.
3. Each control records its side, distance, its distance ratio to the numeral, its count of leading digits shared with the gold, and the factor used.
**Limits stated before the run [I]:** (a) a control near a present numeral may share part of its digits with it and so receive a small boost of its own; this makes the adjusted effect conservative, and is unavoidable for near cues because near-gold numbers are near each other; (b) matching does not cover digit frequency, roundness or numerals near other prompt numerals; (c) the unit is not varied (all candidates carry the target unit), so unit compatibility is a separate contrast that was done in Stage 14.14; (d) controls shared by two numerals of one prompt are scored twice with different `matched_to` fields; (e) ten structures per batch, certificates UNREVIEWED.
**Tests [V]:** `test_matched_distance_s159.py` 8 passed (shape, distance and prefix helpers; controls are same-shape, absent and within the factor; two per side; reasons for no control; determinism and no new leading zero; negative gold; a plan on S06 with unique ids, controls pointing at present numerals and the directory refusal).

### J14.17-002 · 2026-10-21 · decision
kind: decision
**Frozen analysis, decided before any matched-control score exists** (implemented in `s160_matched_distance_analysis.py`; 8 tests on synthetic jobs and records with known effects pass):
1. Adjusted presence effect per numeral = log-probability of the numeral as an answer minus the mean log-probability of its OK matched controls under the same prompt. Roles, structure averaging, paired contrasts and the cluster bootstrap (2,000 resamples, seed 42) are those of `s155`, so the numbers are on the Stage 14.14 scale.
2. Primary: all matched controls. Sensitivity A: only controls with distance ratio within [0.5, 2]. Sensitivity B: only controls sharing the same count of leading digits with the gold as the numeral.
3. Groups: batch A (S01 to S10), batch B (S11 to S20), and the two pooled, labelled descriptive (J14.15-005 keeps the batches separate).
4. When the Stage 14.14 records are supplied: the unadjusted effect of the same batch-A numerals beside the adjusted one, and the present-numeral scores of the two runs compared (same model, dtype, prompts; expected near-identical).
5. **Reading rule:** a role's adjusted effect is "above plausibility" only if its 95% interval excludes zero and the mean is at least 0.4 nats (the 95th percentile of the dtype noise, 0.35 to 0.38, J14.9-004 and J14.15-007). Otherwise it is "not distinguishable from plausibility at this precision", which is not equivalence. A numeral with no OK matched control is NOT_EVALUATED, never zero. No p-values.
6. **What a result can and cannot say, stated now:** even an effect "above plausibility" says only that a numeral that is in the prompt is more probable as an answer than numerals that are equally far from the gold and of the same shape; it does not say the model copies it, or that it is irrelevant to the answer, and no relevance-gating or mechanism statement follows. An effect that is not distinguishable from plausibility would mean the Stage 14.14 boost for cues is largely explained by closeness to the gold.
