### J14.4-005 · 2026-10-16T13:45 · decision
kind: decision
**What:** Predictor ladder and cross-validation design (fixed, stated before looking at results).
- Predictors (P5.3 ladder items 1 to 2 plus the entropy comparator): `metadata_law_cue_domain` (one-hot of
  governing law, cue type, domain); `tfidf_prompt_text` (word 1-2-gram TF-IDF of the variant-0 prompt);
  `prompt_statistics` (length, words, digits, numbers, sentence marks); `fitted_entropy_features` (the two
  stored entropy features); `combined_non_activation` (metadata + statistics + entropy features, cohort only).
- Each is a logistic regression with C=1 and no hyperparameter search, so there is no selection step that could
  leak; preprocessing (one-hot, TF-IDF vocabulary, scaling) is fitted inside each training fold.
- Schemes: leave-one-family-out versus leave-one-cluster-out over the same families, AUROC from pooled
  out-of-fold probabilities (the Phase-I convention). The family-minus-cluster AUROC difference gets a 95%
  percentile interval from 2,000 resamples of whole clusters, seed 42.
- The 135-family cohort for predictors that need entropy; all 140 families for the inclusion-agnostic re-analysis of
  the three predictors that do not.
**Why:** families inside a cluster are not independent (v2 §8.1, p.41: the independent unit is the structure
cluster), so both the held-out unit and the bootstrap unit are clusters.
**Alternatives considered:** tuned or nested hyperparameters (rejected: adds a selection step without need);
re-using the project's `run_balance_audit.py` (rejected: it writes tracked results and filters on a model-name key
absent from the v7 table).
**Pitfall noted:** a leave-out AUROC below 0.5 (for example the `prompt_statistics` predictor) is the known
anti-learning artefact of leaving one unit out with weak features, not a negative signal.

### J14.4-009 · 2026-10-16T14:30 · observation
kind: observation
**What [V] (135-family cohort, 70 clusters, 70 positives; AUROC from pooled out-of-fold probabilities):**

| Predictor | Leave-one-family-out | Leave-one-cluster-out | Family minus cluster [95% cluster bootstrap] |
|---|---|---|---|
| metadata (law, cue, domain) | 0.595 | 0.456 | +0.138 [+0.044, +0.220] |
| prompt text (TF-IDF) | 0.620 | 0.515 | +0.105 [-0.045, +0.239] |
| prompt statistics | 0.469 | 0.385 | +0.083 [+0.003, +0.168] |
| fitted entropy features | 0.729 | 0.726 | +0.003 [-0.005, +0.012] |
| combined non-activation | 0.692 | 0.673 | +0.019 [-0.030, +0.072] |

**Reading it:** the predictors that can only recognise the template (metadata, text) carry a family-held-out signal
that is gone under cluster-held-out folds, while the fitted-entropy features, which do not depend on template
identity, lose nothing. So leave-one-family-out evaluation does reward template recognition. The interval for the
text predictor includes zero; for metadata and statistics it does not. These weak predictors reach 0.60 to 0.62 at
most, far below the stored monitor's 0.76, so template recognition alone does not explain 0.76; whether the
monitor itself loses signal under cluster-held-out folds is NOT_EVALUATED (no activations).
**Caveat:** fixed hyperparameters, one split scheme, exploratory, not preregistered.
