### J15.1-006 · 2026-11-12 · decision
kind: decision
**Analysis rules fixed before any batch-1 record exists [I]** (`scripts/v2/s176_batch1_analysis.py`, tests on synthetic records):
- **Parity** (greedy vLLM vs HF on the same 80 problems): PASS requires graded-correctness agreement (declared view) on at least 85% of shared problems, an accuracy difference of at most 0.05, and completion-status agreement on at least 85%. Exact text identity is reported, not required (bf16 kernels differ between engines; Stage 14 measured 13 of 209 Qwen greedy answers changing between float32 and bfloat16, J14.9-004). On FAIL, vLLM calibration results are not used until the difference is understood.
- **Calibration** (per run): statuses, the three grading views and O2; per structure (instances pooled) the selection share from samples 0-3 and the estimation share from samples 4-7 only; band membership by the selection share in [0.30, 0.85] (the planning band of G2; the final band is fixed in G1 before G3); accuracy by level and domain with a 2,000-resample bootstrap over structures (seed 42). Non-COMPLETE records count as not correct for O1 and are never relabelled wrong.
